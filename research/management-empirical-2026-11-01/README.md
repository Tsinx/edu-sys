# Management empirical topic search

Started: 2026-11-01 (user timezone). Status: active.

## Objective

Find an original, publishable management research question using secondary/open or lawfully collectable data and primarily statistical methods. Start from obtainable data, examine 2026 high-level journal research and the closest prior studies, test a distinct theoretical and empirical increment, and iterate until a defensible project is found. The intended teaching use is a genuine research-to-submission demonstration, rather than a replication presented as a new paper.

## Working gates

1. Obtain actual records and inspect schema, period, unit, identifiers, sample construction and reuse terms.
2. Connect an important management choice to a substantive explanation; write the contribution against named closest studies.
3. Inspect the relevant full texts, not only titles or abstracts, before clearing novelty.
4. Specify a credible statistical design and conduct a bounded pilot, including timing, comparison and alternative explanations.
5. Record candidates continued, rejected and unresolved. A significant coefficient is not a selection criterion.
6. Keep data retrieval, empirical outputs, literature reading and publication-readiness judgments distinct.

## Files

- `records/`: query and decision ledger, source and license receipts.
- `literature/`: verified public papers and reading notes.
- `data/`: obtained data, provenance, hashes and dictionaries.
- `analysis/`: independently written retrieval and statistical probes.

Courseware, prior manuscript projects and unrelated workspace changes are preserved. No submission, author attestation, email or public posting is authorized by this search task.
